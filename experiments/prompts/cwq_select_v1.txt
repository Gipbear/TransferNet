of those paths.

   the answer.

   choose the right tail.

Rules:
- Output only entity names that appear in the paths.
- Never fabricate names or output Freebase ids (m.xxx / g.xxx).
- Do not explain. Output only these two lines:

Supporting Paths: <path numbers>
